    How to play

    Each case is a deck plan of the freighter Shadow Spark. The glowing blue is the mold.

    1. One action per turn. Tap a door or a vent to seal it (uses one seal), switch on the torch and tap a mold cell to burn it to ash, or press Wait.
    2. Then the mold grows into every open neighbouring cell – up, down, left and right. The faint blue dots show where it will grow next.
    3. Vents are connected. If the mold reaches an open vent, next turn it bursts out of every other open vent.
    4. Frost slows it down. Cold storage cells (marked with asterisks) need two turns to be overgrown.
    5. Ash is final. Nothing grows through a burned cell. Infected doors can no longer be sealed – but they can be burned.
    6. Win when the mold has nowhere left to grow. Lose if it touches the bridge, the reactor or the crew quarters.

    Stars: three stars for the smallest possible infection, fewer for a messier containment. Undo takes back one turn; Restart begins the case again.

    About

    Blue Mold: Containment is a small puzzle game set in the world of the sci-fi horror novel Blue Mold by Cal Rennick. A freighter, a frozen container from the station Kallichore-7, sixteen hours to Enceladus – and something blue that keeps growing.

    The game works offline, has no ads, no accounts and no purchases, and collects no data. Your progress is saved only on this device. Privacy policy
