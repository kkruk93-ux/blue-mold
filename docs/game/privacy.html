Privacy policy

Blue Mold: Containment · effective 30 October 2026

Short version: the game collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. Blue Mold: Containment does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies and no in-app purchases.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (which case files you closed and how many stars you earned) is saved locally in your browser's / app's storage on your own device so that you can continue later. It never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with About → Reset progress, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Network access

The game downloads its own files from GitHub Pages (kkruk93-ux.github.io) when it is first opened and then works offline. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver those files; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to YouTube, TikTok, X or Suno on the About screen open those services only if you tap them; their own privacy policies apply there.

Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/kkruk93-ux/blue-mold or use the contact address shown on the game's store listing.
